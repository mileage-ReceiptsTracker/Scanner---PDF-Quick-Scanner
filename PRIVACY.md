# Privacy Policy

**Scanner - PDF Quick Scanner**

Last updated: May 14, 2026

## Overview

Scanner - PDF Quick Scanner ("the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we handle your information.

## Data Collection

We do not collect, store, or share any personal data. The App does not require you to create an account or provide any personal information.

## Scanned Documents

All scanned documents are stored locally on your device only. We do not upload, transmit, or have access to any documents you scan.

## Advertising

The App uses Google AdMob to display ads. AdMob may collect certain device information to show relevant ads. Please refer to [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) for more information.

## Camera Access

The App requests access to your device's camera solely for the purpose of scanning documents. Camera data is not stored or transmitted anywhere.

## Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13.
