# Scanner - PDF Quick Scanner

**Scanner - PDF Quick Scanner** is a free iPhone app that turns your camera into a powerful document scanner. Scan any document instantly and save it as a high-quality PDF — no sign-up, no watermarks, no limits.

---

## Features

- **Fast scanning** — point your camera and scan in seconds
- **Multi-page PDFs** — combine multiple pages into one document
- **High-quality output** — crisp, clear PDFs every time
- **Share anywhere** — email, Messages, AirDrop, Files, iCloud Drive
- **100% Free** — no account, no subscription, no watermarks, ever

## Support

Having an issue or need help? We're here for you.

**Email:** support.scannerapp@gmail.com

Common questions:

**Q: Is the app really free?**
A: Yes, completely free. No hidden fees, no subscription, no watermarks.

**Q: Where are my scans saved?**
A: Scans are saved locally on your iPhone. You can also share them to iCloud Drive or any other app.

**Q: How do I delete a page from a scan?**
A: Open the scan, then tap the red X button on any page to remove it.

**Q: How do I share a PDF?**
A: Open the scan and tap the share icon (top right) to share via email, AirDrop, Messages, or save to Files.

## Privacy

Scanner does not collect any personal data. All scans are stored locally on your device only.
